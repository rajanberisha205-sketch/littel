How Does Our Internet Speed Test Work?

Our free internet speed test measures your real download speed directly in your browser using a small file transferred from a globally distributed server. The test runs for about 4 seconds and reports the average throughput in megabits per second (Mbps), just like fast.com and Speedtest.

Why Test Your Internet Speed?

  • Verify that you're getting the speed you pay for from your ISP.
  • Diagnose slow streaming, buffering, and lag in video calls or games.
  • Compare Wi-Fi vs. ethernet performance in different rooms.
  • Check mobile network speed on 4G and 5G connections.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good internet speed?

For basic browsing: 5 Mbps. For HD streaming: 10 Mbps. For 4K streaming and gaming: 25–50 Mbps. For households with multiple users: 100 Mbps or more.

Is this speed test accurate?

Yes. We measure real-world download throughput with a warm-up phase to skip TCP slow-start, giving you a result that matches what fast.com and Speedtest typically report.

Is the internet speed test free?

Completely free. No signup, no app, no ads blocking your result. Just click "Start Test" and get your speed in seconds.

Tips for the Most Accurate Result

  • Close other tabs, downloads, and streaming apps.
  • Use a wired ethernet connection if possible.
  • Test at different times of day to see peak-hour slowdowns.
  • Restart your router if results seem unusually low.
