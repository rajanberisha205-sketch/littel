About the Fasqoo Lite Internet Speed Test

Fasqoo Lite is a free, browser-based internet speed test that measures your real download and upload speed, latency (ping) and jitter in seconds. It works worldwide on any device — Android, iPhone, tablet, laptop or desktop — with no app installation required.

What does the Fasqoo speed test measure?

  • Download speed (Mbps) — data from the internet to your device.
  • Upload speed (Mbps) — data from your device to the internet.
  • Ping / Latency (ms) — round-trip time for a data packet.
  • Jitter (ms) — variation in ping. Low jitter = stable connection.

How does the measurement work?

Fasqoo Lite connects to the nearest Cloudflare Edge server and uses an adaptive multi-stream engine: it starts with a single data stream and automatically adds more (up to 8) as your measured speed increases. Every value shown on this page comes from real bytes counted in your browser during the test — nothing is estimated or simulated.

What is a good internet speed?

  • 5–10 Mbps — email, browsing, SD video.
  • 15–25 Mbps — HD streaming on one device.
  • 50–100 Mbps — 4K streaming, gaming, multiple users.
  • 200+ Mbps — heavy households, work-from-home.
  • 500+ Mbps — fiber connections, pro users.
